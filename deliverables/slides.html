NGO: Chinese Rainbow Network, volunteer-run. Its Gala needs celebrities, leaders, awardees, panelists and sponsors. Outreach ran on spreadsheets, cold DMs and copy-pasted letters.

Summits: Yale SOM's Responsible AI conference (students change yearly) and the Yale Innovation Summit (2,600+ attendees; 143 speakers in 2024).

Safety

Honest routes, reviewed letters, data not instructions

Caught in the showcase: a scout's guess (Gigi Hsu → Tauhid Zaman → Alondra Nelson) was ranked warm. Now it is a "Suggested intro (unverified)", cold; after a re-plan ($0.024) her official IAS route is #1 and the guess is #3, labelled "Suggested intro via Gigi Hsu (unverified)".

Reviewer: 7 of 12 drafts returned with fixes (unsupported honorific, assumed date stated as planned, "no verified address: do not send").

PASSdocument injection · PASSreply injection · PASSwrong date/link/$3,000 fee · PASSbudget cap

Also: a source on every contact, public photos only from Wikimedia Commons, an organiser–guest merge guard, a never-out-anyone rule, and no send capability.

What's next

Honest limits and the next three builds

Limits today
  • Web search is 80–85% of cost, and it varies
  • Wikidata and Commons only know notable people
  • WeChat has no export; team contact lists are imported by hand
  • Drafts still need a human; the UI is English-only
  • Local Drive folder only, no Gmail sync
Next
  • Shared verification cache (≈−40% at scale)
  • Gmail and Drive connectors, still never auto-send
  • Chinese UI and WeChat chat import
  • More user tests with NGO and summit teams
