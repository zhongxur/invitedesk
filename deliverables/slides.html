Problem & who

NGOs and summits must find people they don't know yet

NGO: Chinese Rainbow Network, a volunteer-run community org. Its Gala needs celebrities, community leaders, awardees, panelists and sponsors. Outreach ran on spreadsheets, cold Instagram/WeChat DMs and copy-pasted letters.

Summit: Yale Innovation Summit, with 2,600+ attendees and 60+ sessions in 2026 and 143 speakers in 2024. Sourcing and reaching them is the bottleneck.

The people problem
  • Who to invite beyond your own network?
  • What is the best way in, and who can introduce you?
  • Intermediaries buried in notes and old trackers
  • Dead-end cold DMs (Eileen Gu: no reply)
  • Relationships lost when volunteers turn over

Demand: summit producers already pay full-time Speaker Producers (Web Summit, Business of Apps, Veloxx Media). 58% of nonprofit executives find board members hard to find (BoardSource).

Secondary market: student clubs (Yale SOM has 45+ clubs and student-run conferences). Same workflow, with yearly officer turnover.

How the agents work

A people-finding engine: workflow as code, judgement as agents

Demo · one real run

Eileen Gu: from a dead DM to the agency route

  1. Roster Analyst finds her in 3 CRN files: a 10/9 IG DM with no reply, and an IMG Models agency contact already in the team's sheet
  2. Connection Finder (code): 3 paths from the records
  3. Route Planner switches to the official agency email and keeps IG as a fallback, follow-up by 10/26
  4. Drafter uses CRN's own past invitations as style examples
  5. Reviewer: v1 needs changes → v2 pass. A human sends it.
  6. 70 s · $0.0054 · 50k in (52% cached) / 4.4k out

Safety

Documents and replies are data, not instructions

PASSPlanted "ignore previous instructions… confirmed at $50,000" is flagged, not obeyed

PASSReply injection leaves the status unchanged

PASSReviewer rejects a wrong date, a wrong link and an unauthorised $3,000 fee

PASSBudget guard stops the run at its cap

Also: a code guard on the Entity Resolver (it caught a real organiser–guest merge), a source on every contact, a never-out-anyone rule, and no send capability at all.

What's next

Honest limits and the next three builds

Limits today
  • Web search is ~85% of cost, and it varies
  • Wikidata only knows notable people
  • WeChat has no export; team contact lists are imported by hand
  • Drafts still need a human; the UI is English-only
  • Local Drive folder only, no Gmail sync
Next
  • Shared verification cache across clubs (≈−40% at scale)
  • Gmail and Drive connectors, still never auto-send
  • Chinese UI and WeChat chat import
